Política de Privacidade
개인정보 처리방침

We believe privacy should be simple. Here's everything we do — and don't do — with your data.

🙅 No Account, No Personal Data

Korean School does not require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect your name, email address, date of birth, or any other personal information. You are completely anonymous to us.

💾 Progress Stored Locally — Your Browser Only

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, and preferences are saved using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device — it is never sent to any server, never shared, and never seen by us. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

📢 Advertising

This site is free and supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show you relevant ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of some other third-party vendors' use of advertising cookies on the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

🔮 Upcoming Premium Features

We plan to introduce an AI Text-to-Speech (TTS) feature as a paid premium option. If and when user accounts are introduced for paid tiers, this policy will be updated to clearly describe what additional data is collected. You will be notified of any meaningful changes before they take effect.

📊 Analytics — Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how learners use the site — such as which lessons are popular and which pages are most helpful. This involves aggregated, anonymised data (page views, session duration, general region). No personally identifiable information is collected. Google Analytics sets cookies; you can opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or your browser's privacy settings.

✉️ Contact Email

The email address listed on our Contact page is provided for voluntary communication only. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not share your email with third parties. See the prohibition notice below regarding unauthorized automated collection.

📝 Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be noted on this page with an updated effective date. We will not notify you by email (since we don't have your email), so we encourage you to revisit this page occasionally.

Effective date: 2026-06-04

This website explicitly prohibits the unauthorized collection of any email address published on this site using email harvesting programs or any other technical device.
