Themed Travel in Korea

Travel by passion — whether you're a food lover, festival-hopper, history buff, or outdoor adventurer. Korea has a dedicated route for every type of traveller.

1 Food Tour · 음식 여행

Korean cuisine is one of the world's greatest — and the best way to experience it is to eat like a local. From dawn market breakfasts to midnight 포장마차 (street stalls), every meal is an event.

Korea's festival calendar is packed year-round. Some of the world's most extraordinary cultural events happen here — from mud wrestling to lantern-lit rivers, cherry blossom seasons, and ancestral ceremonies unchanged for 500 years.

🌟 Major Festivals in Detail · 주요 축제
April · 4월
🌸 진해 군항제
Jinhae Cherry Blossom Festival
Korea's largest cherry blossom festival — 360,000 trees bloom across the naval city of Jinhae. One of Asia's most spectacular spring events. Free entry, train access from Masan.
May · 5월
🏮 연등회
Lotus Lantern Festival (UNESCO)
Thousands of glowing lanterns parade through Seoul's Jongno for Buddha's Birthday. UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage since 2020. The lantern parade and folk performances are free.
July · 7월
💧 보령 머드 축제
Boryeong Mud Festival
Korea's most famous festival for international visitors — wallow in the famous Boryeong grey mud at Daecheon Beach. K-pop concerts, mud wrestling, and waterslides. Runs 10 days in mid-July.
September · 9월
🌕 추석
Chuseok — Korean Thanksgiving
Korea's most important traditional holiday — a 3-day harvest celebration with ancestral rites, 송편 (rice cake) making, and traditional games. Many restaurants close; families gather. Gyeongbokgung is free during Chuseok.
October · 10월
🎬 부산국제영화제
Busan International Film Festival
Asia's premier film festival — held for 10 days at Haeundae and BIFF Square. Korean films, world premieres, and K-star sightings. Tickets available online from late September.
November · 11월
🍂 단풍 시즌
Autumn Foliage Season
Korea's mountains explode in crimson and gold. Best spots: Seoraksan (설악산, late Oct), Naejangsan (내장산, early Nov), Gyeryongsan (계룡산, mid Nov). Book accommodation months ahead.
💡
Festival Korean Phrases 축제 표현

3 Sightseeing · 관광

Korea's sightseeing ranges from 1,300-year-old Buddhist temples to space-age architecture. The country has 15 UNESCO World Heritage Sites and hundreds of national treasures. Here's the essential guide.

🏛️ UNESCO World Heritage Sites · 유네스코 세계유산
경주 · UNESCO
🛕 불국사 & 석굴암
Bulguksa Temple + Seokguram Grotto
Two masterpieces of 8th-century Buddhist architecture. The stone pagodas of Bulguksa and the serene granite Buddha of Seokguram are considered Korea's finest artistic achievements.
수원 · UNESCO
🏰 화성
Hwaseong Fortress
A 5.7 km fortress wall incorporating scientific military innovations of the 18th century. Built in just 28 months (1794–1796) by King Jeongjo. Walkable in 2 hours with spectacular views.
창덕궁 · UNESCO
🌿 창덕궁 후원
Changdeokgung Secret Garden
A Joseon palace built in 1405 with a stunning 300-year-old landscaped garden. The Secret Garden (후원) tour is guided and limited — book ahead. Best in autumn when the foliage is golden.
제주 · UNESCO
🌋 제주 화산섬과 용암동굴
Jeju Volcanic Island & Lava Tubes
Hallasan volcano, Seongsan Ilchulbong crater, and Manjanggul lava tube — all in one. Korea's only natural UNESCO World Heritage Site. The 9.6 km lava tube is one of the world's longest.
서울 · UNESCO
🏯 조선 왕릉
Royal Tombs of Joseon Dynasty
40 royal tombs spread across the Seoul metropolitan area — serene forested grounds where Korea's kings rest. Seleon (선릉), Heonneung (헌릉), and Donggu Neung (동구릉) are the most accessible.
안동 · UNESCO
🏘️ 하회마을
Hahoe Folk Village
A living Joseon-era village where descendants of the Ryu clan still reside in 600-year-old thatched and tile-roofed homes. Traditional mask dances (하회탈) performed on weekends.

4 Cross-Country · 장거리

Korea's terrain is 70% mountains and laced with rivers and coastline — making it a paradise for walkers, cyclists, and hikers. The government has invested billions in long-distance trails and dedicated bicycle paths.

🚶 Walking Routes · 도보 여행
🧱
한양도성 순성길
Hanyang City Wall Trail — Seoul
18.6 km Full day Easy–Moderate
Walk the full circumference of Seoul's 600-year-old Joseon city wall — from Gyeongbokgung through Bugaksan mountain, down to Heunginjimun gate, around Namsan, and back. Four sections can be walked separately. The Bugaksan section offers the best Seoul panoramas. Free entry.

🌊
제주 올레길
Jeju Olle Trail — Complete Jeju Circumnavigation
437 km total 26 sections Any difficulty
Korea's most famous long-distance trail — 26 sections circling all of Jeju Island through volcanic coastline, bamboo forests, haenyeo villages, and 오름 craters. Each section is 10–20 km and can be done independently. Section 7 (서귀포 오름 코스) is the most dramatic. Olle Guide offices stamp your passport at each start.

Olle Phrase 올레 표현

🚲 Bicycle Routes · 자전거 여행
🚲
국토종주 자전거길
4 Rivers Cross-Country Bike Path
633 km Incheon → Busan 7–10 days
Korea's ultimate cycling achievement — a dedicated, paved bicycle path from Incheon on the Yellow Sea all the way to Busan on the South Sea, following the Han, Nakdong, Geum, and Yeongsan rivers. Stamping stations at each major waypoint. Completing the full route earns an official certificate and medal from the Korea Tourism Organization.

한강 자전거도로
Han River Bike Path — Seoul
150 km network Flat Beginner-friendly
Seoul's Han River has 40 km of dedicated bike paths on both banks — flat, paved, and fully separated from traffic. Rent a bike at any of the 10+ riverside parks (₩3,000/hr). The stretch from Yeouido to Ttukseom is most scenic with sunset views of the city bridges.
⛰️ Mountain Hikes · 등산
제주 · 1,950m
🏔️ 한라산
Hallasan — Jeju's Sleeping Volcano
Korea's highest peak. Seongpanak trail (13.5 km, 6–8 hrs round trip) reaches the crater lake 백록담. Eorimok trail (8.6 km, 4–5 hrs) is shorter. Summit closes at 12:00 PM — start early.
속초 · 1,708m
🗻 설악산
Seoraksan — Granite Giant
Korea's most dramatic mountain — jagged granite peaks, waterfalls, and the Ulsanbawi rock formation (873 stone steps). Autumn foliage is extraordinary. Biseondae Valley trail is superb for day hikers.
서울 · 836m
🌲 도봉산
Dobongsan — Seoul's Granite Crown
Only 40 minutes by subway from central Seoul. Jagged granite peaks visible from much of the city. The Uiam trail leads to Jaunbong peak (740m) with stunning views over the entire Seoul basin.
전남 · 1,915m
⛰️ 지리산
Jirisan — Korea's Spiritual Mountain
Korea's first national park (designated 1967) and longest ridge walk — 25 km connecting 10 peaks along the Baekdu-daegan spine. The two-day Cheonwangbong summit hike (1,915m) is a rite of passage for Korean hikers.

💡
Hiking Culture in Korea 등산 문화
Koreans take hiking seriously — brightly coloured technical gear is mandatory fashion. Mountain huts (산장) serve 막걸리 (rice wine), ramen, and 김밥 at the top of most peaks. Many trails are well-marked with distance and time signs in both Korean and English. National parks charge a small entrance fee (₩1,000–3,500).
