Getting Around · 서울 교통

Seoul's subway covers the entire city and beyond. Get a T-Money card (티머니) at any convenience store — top it up and use it on all buses, subways, and some convenience stores. Standard fare is ₩1,400–2,000 per trip. Taxis start at ₩4,800 base fare. KakaoTaxi is the easiest way to hail one.

Fortress Walk Tip 화성 산책 팁

The full 5.7 km fortress wall walk takes about 2 hours at a relaxed pace. Start from Paldalmun Gate (팔달문) and walk counter-clockwise for the best light. The 화성열차 (Hwaseong Train) also does a circuit for those who prefer not to walk. Entry: ₩1,500.

Getting Around Jeju · 제주 교통

Renting a car is by far the best way to explore Jeju — attractions are spread across the island and buses are infrequent. International driving licence accepted. Motorbike and e-bike rentals are also popular. For hiking Hallasan: the Seongpanak trail (13.5 km round trip) is most scenic; the shorter Eorimok trail (8.6 km) is suitable for beginners.

Hallasan Hiking 한라산 등산

Summit access (윗세오름 crater) via Seongpanak trail closes at 12:00 PM — arrive before 8:00 AM. Bring warm layers (even in summer), 2L water, and snacks. Entry is free. The crater lake 백록담 is spectacular.

7 Other Cities · 기타 도시

Korea rewards travellers who venture beyond the classic route. These four cities each offer a distinct experience that few tourists discover.

전주
Jeonju — Birthplace of Bibimbap

Korea's designated slow-food city. Home to the nation's largest intact 한옥 village, the best bibimbap (비빔밥) in the country, and extraordinary 막걸리 (rice wine) bars. The Friday/Saturday night market is a bucket-list food experience.

2.5h bus from Seoul
735 hanok homes in the village
속초
Sokcho — Gateway to Seoraksan

A small coastal city that serves as the launching point for Seoraksan National Park (설악산) — Korea's most dramatic mountain landscape with granite peaks, waterfalls, and ancient temples. The waterfront seafood market is exceptional.

2.5h express bus from Seoul
1,708m peak Daecheongbong summit
담양
Damyang — Bamboo Forest Town

Famous for its vast 메타세쿼이아 (metasequoia) tree-lined road and Korea's most celebrated bamboo grove (죽녹원). The slow-food village serves 대통밥 (bamboo tube rice) and fresh rice noodles. Only 30 minutes from Gwangju.

30 min from Gwangju
Bamboo forest + tree avenue
통영
Tongyeong — Korea's Naples

A stunning harbour city on Korea's southern coast — islands scattered in turquoise water, fresh oysters by the kilo, and cable cars up to panoramic ocean views. The birthplace of composer Yun I-sang and painter Jeon Hyuk-lim.

3.5h express bus from Seoul
Oyster capital of Korea
