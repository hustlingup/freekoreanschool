Day 1 Seoul Full Day
☀️08:00
Breakfast at Gwangjang Market · 광장시장
광장시장 아침 식사
Korea's oldest traditional market. Try 빈대떡 (mung bean pancakes) and 마약 김밥 (addictive kimbap) — iconic Seoul street breakfast.
Say: 빈대떡 하나 주세요 (Bindaetteok hana juseyo) — One mung bean pancake please
🏯09:30
Gyeongbokgung Palace · 경복궁
경복궁 관람 및 수문장 교대식
Arrive early for the 10:00 Royal Guard Changing Ceremony (수문장 교대식). Rent a 한복 (hanbok) at the palace gate and entry is free — stunning photo opportunity.
Say: 입장료가 얼마예요? (Ipjangnyo ga eolmayeyo?) — How much is admission?
🎨11:30
Bukchon Hanok Village · 북촌 한옥마을
북촌 한옥마을 산책
Walk 15 minutes from Gyeongbokgung through Seoul's most photogenic traditional village — 600-year-old hanok houses on winding alleys. Please be quiet for residents.
Say: 조용히 해주세요 (Joyonghi haejuseyo) — Please be quiet
🍱13:00
Lunch in Insadong · 인사동 점심
인사동 한정식 또는 비빔밥
Head to Insadong for lunch. Try a 한정식 (Korean set meal) or a bowl of 비빔밥 in one of the traditional restaurants. Browse the craft shops afterward.
Say: 비빔밥 하나 주세요 (Bibimbap hana juseyo) — One bibimbap please
🗼15:00
N Seoul Tower · 남산타워
남산타워 전망대
Take the cable car up Namsan mountain for panoramic Seoul views. The Love Lock fence at the base is iconic. Observatory entry is optional — the free outdoor deck is excellent.
Say: 케이블카 타는 곳이 어디예요? (Keibeuulka taneun gosi eodiyeyo?) — Where do I board the cable car?
🛍️17:00
Myeongdong Shopping · 명동 쇼핑
명동 거리 쇼핑 및 간식
Hit Myeongdong for K-Beauty shopping and street food snacks — 호떡 (sweet pancake), 떡볶이 (spicy rice cakes), and 닭꼬치 (chicken skewers). All under ₩5,000 each.
Say: 이거 써도 돼요? (Igeo sseo-do daeyo?) — Can I try this?
🌃19:00
Dinner & Han River · 한강 저녁
한강 치맥
Join locals for the ultimate Seoul evening: 치맥 (fried chicken + beer) at Han River Park. Order delivery via 배달의민족 app or buy from the riverside kiosks at Yeouido or Banpo Park.
Say: 치킨 한 마리 주세요 (Chikin han mari juseyo) — One whole chicken please
💡
T-Money Card 티머니
Buy a T-Money card (₩4,000) at any convenience store (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven) and charge it to pay for subway, bus, and even some convenience stores. Saves time at every stop.

2 2 Days: Seoul Essentials · 1박 2일

2 DAYS

Easy ₩80,000–150,000 total
1 night
Stay
Seoul
Base city
Day 1 Palace Row + Hongdae Night
🏯09:00
Changdeokgung Palace & Secret Garden · 창덕궁 후원
Book the Secret Garden tour (비원) in advance — the guided walk through 300-year-old landscaped gardens runs hourly and is absolutely stunning. English tours available.
Say: 후원 투어 예약했어요 (Huwon tuyeo yeyakhaesseoyo) — I reserved the Secret Garden tour
🍱12:30
Jongno Lunch · 종로 점심
Try 삼계탕 (ginseng chicken soup) at one of the famous restaurants near Anguk station — hearty, healthy, and iconic for first-time visitors.
Say: 삼계탕 한 그릇 주세요 (Samgyetang han geureut juseyo) — One bowl of ginseng chicken soup please
🌿14:00
Cheonggyecheon Stream Walk · 청계천
Walk along Seoul's beautiful urban stream from Gwanghwamun toward Dongdaemun — 5.8 km of peaceful greenery cutting through the heart of the city.
Say: 청계천이 어디에 있어요? (Cheonggyecheoni eodie isseoyo?) — Where is Cheonggyecheon?
🎵18:00
Hongdae Evening · 홍대 저녁
Hongdae is Seoul's youth arts and nightlife hub. Street performers fill the squares from 7 PM. Eat dinner at a Korean BBQ restaurant, then explore clubs, indie cafés, and arcade bars.
Say: 삼겹살 2인분 주세요 (Samgyeopsal i-inbun juseyo) — Two portions of pork belly please
Day 2 Suwon Fortress Day Trip
🚄09:00
Train to Suwon · 수원행 기차
Take the 1호선 (Line 1) subway from Seoul Station — 50 minutes to Suwon Station. Or catch a train on the Gyeongbu line (35 minutes). No reservation needed.
Say: 수원 가는 열차가 몇 번이에요? (Suwon ganeun yeolcha ga myeot beonieyo?) — Which platform for the Suwon train?
🏰10:30
Hwaseong Fortress Wall Walk · 화성 성곽길
Walk the full 5.7 km UNESCO fortress wall — took 28 months to build in 1796. Amazing views of the old city. The Hwahongmun water gate is the best photo spot.
Say: 성곽 걷는 길이 어디예요? (Seonggwak geotneun giri eodiyeyo?) — Where is the fortress wall path?
🥩13:00
Suwon Galbi Lunch · 수원 갈비
Suwon is famous for 왕갈비 (king galbi) — huge beef short ribs grilled tableside. Head to the Hyanggyo-gil galbi alley for authentic restaurants that have been operating for decades.
Say: 갈비 1인분에 얼마예요? (Galbi il-inbuneseo eolmayeyo?) — How much is one portion of galbi?
🎎15:00
Korean Folk Village · 한국민속촌
30 minutes by bus from Suwon — a living museum of 260 traditional structures from the Joseon period. Craftsmen demonstrate weaving, pottery, and tightrope walking. Magical at sunset.
Say: 민속촌 가는 버스가 뭐예요? (Minsokcchon ganeun beoseu ga mwoyeyo?) — Which bus goes to the Folk Village?

3 3 Days: Seoul + Gyeongju · 2박 3일

서울 + 경주 2박 3일

1 Seoul, 1 Gyeongju

Seoul↔Gyeongju
Day 1Seoul: Palace + Markets + Nightlife
🏯09:00
Gyeongbokgung + Bukchon
Royal palace (with guard ceremony at 10:00) followed by the hanok village walk toward Samcheongdong café street.
Say: 한복 빌릴 수 있어요? (Hanbok billil su isseoyo?) — Can I rent a hanbok?
🍜13:00
Lunch: Naengmyeon at Ojang-dong
Try 함흥냉면 (Hamheung cold noodles) — chewy buckwheat noodles in a spicy or broth base. A summer staple that's served all year.
🌸14:30
Namsan Park Hike · 남산 산책
Walk up Namsan from Myeongdong via the forest path (45 min). N Seoul Tower at the summit. Excellent city panorama — especially as dusk approaches.
🛍️17:30
Dongdaemun Design Plaza · DDP
Zaha Hadid's futuristic landmark glows at night. The surrounding fashion market opens from 8 PM — wholesale and retail clothing at excellent prices.
Day 2KTX to Gyeongju: Temples + Tombs
🚄07:30
KTX Seoul → Gyeongju
2-hour journey on KTX. Book at least the day before via Korail app. Alight at 신경주 (Singyeongju) station, then local bus to the city centre (30 min).
Say: 신경주 가는 KTX 표 주세요 (Singyeongju ganeun KTX pyo juseyo) — One KTX ticket to Singyeongju please
🛕10:30
Bulguksa Temple · 불국사
One of Korea's most important Buddhist temples — UNESCO World Heritage. Built in 751, it sits halfway up Tohamsan mountain. The stone pagodas Dabotap and Seokgatap are extraordinary.
Say: 불국사까지 얼마나 걸려요? (Bulguksakkaji eolmana geollyeoyo?) — How long does it take to get to Bulguksa?
⛰️12:30
Seokguram Grotto · 석굴암
15-minute bus above Bulguksa. The 8th-century granite Buddha statue inside the artificial cave is one of Korea's greatest artistic achievements. Serene and deeply moving.
🍱14:00
Lunch + 황남빵 · 경주 전통 점심
Eat near Bulguksa then head to 황리단길 (Hwangridangil) — Gyeongju's trendy café street lined with traditional hanok buildings. Buy the famous 황남빵 red bean pastries.
🏛️15:30
Tumuli Park Royal Tombs · 대릉원
Walk among the vast burial mounds of Silla kings — some over 20 meters high. Enter Cheonmachong tomb to see the original royal burial goods. Magic hour light is stunning here.
Day 3Gyeongju Countryside + Return
🌅07:00
Sunrise at Cheomseongdae · 첨성대
The world's oldest surviving astronomical observatory (built 632 AD) is surrounded by flower fields — spectacular at sunrise before tour buses arrive.
🚲09:00
Bicycle Tour · 자전거 여행
Rent a bicycle near Gyeongju city centre (₩3,000/hr) and pedal through the UNESCO historic zone past tombs, temples, and lotus ponds. The route to Anapji Pond is flat and beautiful.
Say: 자전거 빌릴 수 있어요? (Jajeongeo billil su isseoyo?) — Can I rent a bicycle?
🚄14:00
KTX Back to Seoul
Afternoon train returns you to Seoul in 2 hours. Enough time for a final bowl of 삼겹살 in the evening before departure.

5-Day Structure Overview
Days 1–2: Seoul (palaces, markets, Han River, Hongdae) → Day 3: KTX to Busan AM + Gamcheon Village PM → Day 4: Haeundae Beach + Jagalchi Market + Gwangalli night → Day 5: BIFF Square + Beomeosa Temple + evening train home
Day 3KTX to Busan + Gamcheon Village
🚄07:30
KTX Seoul → Busan (2h 15min)
Book in advance. Grab 김밥 (kimbap) from the station convenience store for the train. Arrive at 부산역 (Busan Station) by 10:00.
Say: 부산역까지 KTX 주세요 (Busanyeok kkaji KTX juseyo) — KTX to Busan Station please
🎨10:30
Gamcheon Culture Village · 감천문화마을
Pastel-painted houses cascade down a hillside — Korea's most colourful neighbourhood. Art installations, tiny cafés, and murals around every turn. Arrive before noon to beat crowds.
Say: 감천문화마을에 어떻게 가요? (Gamcheon munhwa maeule eotteoke gayo?) — How do I get to Gamcheon Village?
🐟14:00
Jagalchi Fish Market · 자갈치 시장
Korea's largest seafood market. Buy raw seafood on the ground floor and have it cooked upstairs for a small fee. Try 꽃게 (blue crab), 전복 (abalone), or 성게 (sea urchin).
Say: 이거 구워주세요 (Igeo guwojuseyo) — Please grill this for me
🌉19:00
Gwangalli Beach at Night · 광안리 야경
Gwangalli is famous for the illuminated Gwangan Bridge reflected in the sea. Eat 돼지국밥 (pork rice soup) at a nearby restaurant before settling on the beach with a beer.
Days 4–5Busan: Beach + Temple + Culture
🏖️08:00
Haeundae Beach · 해운대 해수욕장
Korea's most famous beach. Morning walk before crowds. Nearby 해리단길 (Haeridangil) has great breakfast cafés. Try 씨앗호떡 (seed pancakes) from street vendors.
🛕11:00
Haedong Yonggungsa Temple · 해동 용궁사
Korea's only seaside Buddhist temple — dramatically set on coastal rocks with waves crashing below. Accessible by bus from Haeundae (40 min). Free entry.
🎬14:00
BIFF Square + Gukje Market · 국제시장
Walk Busan International Film Festival Square — embedded with handprints of Korean stars. Gukje Market next door sells street food, antiques, and local goods at great prices.
Say: 씨앗호떡 얼마예요? (Ssiat hotteok eolmayeyo?) — How much are the seed pancakes?
🚆17:00
Return KTX to Seoul (Day 5)
Late afternoon KTX has plenty of seats. Stop at Lotteria inside Busan Station for a final 돈카츠 (tonkatsu) or grab convenience store kimbap for the ride home.

7-Day Route Overview
D1–2 Seoul (palaces, markets, Hongdae) → D3 KTX to Gyeongju (Bulguksa, tombs, Cheomseongdae) → D4 Busan (Gamcheon, Jagalchi, Haeundae) → D5 Fly to Jeju (Seongsan Ilchulbong sunrise, black pig dinner) → D6 Jeju (Hallasan hike or Manjanggul cave + O'Sulloc tea fields) → D7 Fly Jeju → Seoul, depart
💡
Jeju Tip 제주 팁
Book Jeju domestic flights at least 2–3 weeks ahead (via Air Seoul, Jeju Air, or T'way). Rental cars are essential on Jeju — public buses are slow. International licence accepted.

10-Day Route Overview
D1–3 Seoul (all highlights + DMZ day trip option) → D4 KTX to Jeonju (hanok village, bibimbap, makgeolli bars) → D5 Bus to Gyeongju (temples, tombs, bicycle tour) → D6–7 Busan (beaches, markets, Haedong temple, Beomeosa) → D8–9 Jeju (Hallasan hike, Seongsan, west coast drive, 오름 volcano) → D10 Fly Jeju → home or Seoul depart
💡
Jeonju 전주 — Don't Miss It
Jeonju is the birthplace of 비빔밥 (bibimbap) and has Korea's largest intact 한옥 (hanok) village. The night market (남부시장 야시장) every Friday and Saturday is extraordinary. Stay one night in a hanok guesthouse.

KORAIL Pass 코레일 패스
For 2-week travellers, consider the KORAIL Pass — unlimited KTX travel for a fixed period. Available for 2, 3, 4, or 5 days within a month. Saves significantly versus individual tickets. Purchase before arriving in Korea.

Month-Long Structure (Sample)
Week 1 Seoul base — neighbourhood deep dives (Itaewon, Seongsu, Mangwon, Yeonnam, Jamsil) → Week 2 Slow travel north — DMZ, Sokcho (Seoraksan hike), Gangneung (coffee street), Gangwondo coast → Week 3 Jeonju → Damyang (bamboo forest) → Yeosu → Tongyeong (Korea's Naples) → Busan → Week 4 Gyeongju bicycle circuit → Jeju Island (rent a scooter, visit all 오름 craters) → final days Seoul
💡
한달살기 (Handalsal-gi) — Monthly Living Tips
Rent a 고시원 (goshiwon, ₩300,000–500,000/month) or 에어비앤비 monthly for budget stays. Use 당근마켓 (Carrot Market) app to buy/sell second-hand goods. Get a Korean SIM card at Incheon Airport for unlimited data. Open a 카카오뱅크 (Kakao Bank) account for local bank transfers. Enrol in a neighbourhood 어학당 (language school) for structured Korean lessons.
ℹ️
Visa Note 비자 안내
Most nationalities get 90 days visa-free. For longer stays: apply for a Working Holiday Visa (18–30 years old, available for 24+ countries), a Language Study Visa (D-4), or a Digital Nomad Visa (introduced 2024). Check the Korean Embassy website for current requirements.
