Travel in practice · 15 minute study

Plan and explain your first Seoul subway journey

A station name is only one part of a useful journey plan. You also need the direction, any transfer, and the exit at the destination. This guide teaches a checking routine and a few Korean questions. It does not provide a live route, fare, timetable, or accessibility guarantee.

Write a journey you can check

Choose two stations for a practice trip. In a current journey planner, record the departure station, destination station, line or lines, transfer station if any, and destination exit. Check the direction shown for the platform against the next stations in the route. A line’s color is useful, but the line number and station names give you another way to confirm the route.

For example, a note might read “depart: [station A] → transfer: [station B] → arrive: [station C] → exit: 2.” The brackets deliberately leave the journey open: fill them from the route you actually checked. A static lesson cannot confirm the best route today, a last train, a closed exit, or whether an elevator is operating. Use the Seoul Metropolitan Government subway page as an official starting point for network information.

Separate three decisions

Word on a signWhat it helps you decideQuestion to ask yourself
방면Direction toward a named placeDoes this platform go toward the stations in my route?
환승Transfer to another service or lineAm I changing lines here, or leaving the station?
출구An exit from the stationWhich exit gets me closer to my destination outside?
충전Adding stored value, in the card contextDo I need to top up this card before travelling?

A destination and an exit are different pieces of information. 서울역 identifies Seoul Station; 2번 출구 identifies Exit 2. Reaching the correct station does not tell you which street you will emerge on. If you need step-free access, check an appropriate elevator route as a separate requirement rather than assuming the nearest exit works.

Understand what tapping is for

Seoul’s official subway guidance explains that its integrated fare calculation depends on tapping when boarding and leaving each mode of transport. Build the habit of following the entry and exit readers rather than assuming one tap covers the entire journey. A successful tap and the route you planned are separate checks: payment alone does not confirm that you chose the correct platform.

The Korea Tourism Organization’s transportation-card guide describes rechargeable cards and where they can be obtained. Before buying a card or pass, check its current coverage, recharge methods, and restrictions. A stored-value card and a time-limited pass are different products; this lesson does not select one for your budget. It intentionally avoids quoting a fare that may have changed.

Ask a question that someone can answer

Constructed sceneKoreanMeaning
Checking a train이 열차는 서울역에 가요?Does this train go to Seoul Station?
Checking a transfer어디에서 갈아타요?Where do I transfer?
Finding an exit2번 출구는 어디에 있어요?Where is Exit 2?
Requesting a repeat다시 말해 주세요.Please say it again.

In 서울역에 가요, 에 marks the destination. In 어디에서 갈아타요?, 에서 marks the place where the transfer action happens. You are giving the location two different grammatical jobs. Practise with 부산역에 가요 to change the destination while keeping the pattern.

If you are unsure, show the written destination and route rather than trying to guess from a fast reply. Ask staff before passing a gate you do not understand. This example does not describe a particular train service: confirm the actual route before boarding.

Use a recovery routine

If the next station does not match the route, check the direction and stop to re-plan. A simple question is 반대 방향이에요? (“Is it the opposite direction?”). Avoid rushing through a gate to chase a train while you are still uncertain about the route. Check the station signs and ask staff how to reach the other platform.

For a low card balance, 어디에서 충전해요? asks where to top up. The answer may name a machine or another location, so keep the repeat phrase ready. Do not infer that a failed tap always means a low balance; a reader or card issue may require staff assistance.

Before a late journey, check a current service timetable. Before a journey with luggage or mobility needs, check a usable elevator route. Before buying an airport or intercity ticket, check that service’s own rules. Those are separate decisions that the city subway vocabulary does not settle.

Check the decision behind the sign

1. Your route changes lines inside the station. Which sign are you looking for?

Answer and explanation

환승 환승 means transfer. 출구 points toward an exit; leaving may take you away from an internal transfer route.

2. Complete 서울역___ 가요, with Seoul Station as the destination.

Answer and explanation

에 에 marks the destination in this pattern. 에서 would describe where an action happens.

3. You reached the right station. What still needs checking?

Answer and explanation

The exit and any access requirements. Station arrival and the route outside are separate. Exit numbers and elevator locations can matter.

4. Can this static lesson confirm the last train tonight?

Answer and explanation

No; check a current service timetable. The revision date records this lesson’s content work. It is not a live transport feed.

Make it yours

Write down a real journey from a current planner and label the departure, direction, transfer, and exit. Then say two help questions aloud. Check that your note still makes sense without the map open. This is a planning exercise; do the current service checks before travelling.
