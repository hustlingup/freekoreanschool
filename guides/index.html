Use the guides alongside the lessons

The guides make connections; the lessons provide more repetition. Start with Hangul if you cannot yet read the examples. Use grammar to expand a sentence pattern and dialogues to practise responses. Your lesson progress and this plan’s ticks stay in your browser rather than an account.

Before checking an answer, say why you chose it. Then change a noun, quantity, or situation and try again. If you can only repeat the original, return to its explanation. The answer panels also work without JavaScript.
