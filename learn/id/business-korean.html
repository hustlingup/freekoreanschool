Bahasa Korea Bisnis: 비즈니스 한국어

🏢 Ungkapan di tempat kerja 📊 Tingkat Lanjut 🔖 Dunia profesional 🤝 Etiket bisnis

Budaya bisnis Korea sangat menjunjung hierarki, formalitas, dan rasa hormat. Whether you are joining a Korean company, working with Korean partners, or attending a business meeting in Korea, mastering professional Korean (비즈니스 한국어) will set you apart. The formal speech level (격식체) is essential — never use casual speech (반말) in a business setting.

1 Formal Speech in Business (격식체)

Perusahaan Korea menggunakan formal polite speech (격식체/합쇼체) — the most respectful register. This is more formal than everyday 존댓말 (해요체). Mastering these endings is non-negotiable in professional settings.

ArtiPolite (everyday 해요체)Formal (business 합쇼체)

saya makan먹어요 먹습니다

💡
Pola utamanya
Dalam ragam formal, verba berakhiran -습니다/-ㅂ니다 (kalimat pernyataan) dan -습니까/-ㅂ니까 (questions). Replace the -(아/어)요 ending you use in daily conversation. This instantly communicates professionalism.

2 Business Greetings (비즈니스 인사)

First impressions in Korean business settings are critical. A deep bow (고개를 숙이다) accompanies formal greetings. Learn these phrases to make a strong professional impression.

3 Business Card Exchange (명함 교환)

Exchanging business cards (명함) is a formal ritual in Korean business culture. Cards are presented and received with kedua tangan, sambil sedikit membungkuk. Jangan pernah menulis di atas kartu nama atau menyimpannya di saku belakang — hal itu dianggap tidak menghormati.

4 Korean Business Hierarchy & Job Titles (직급)

Korean companies follow a strict hierarchy. Understanding titles (직급) is essential for addressing colleagues correctly — always use their title, not their first name.

💡
Menyapa dengan jabatan
In Korea, you address colleagues by their title + 님: 부장님, 과장님. Gunakan nama seseorang hanya jika ia secara tegas mempersilakan Anda. Menyebut nama depan seseorang dalam konteks bisnis tanpa dipersilakan dianggap tidak sopan.

7 Email & Written Communication (이메일)

Korean business emails follow strict conventions. Always open with a respectful greeting, state your purpose clearly, and close with a polite sign-off. Written Korean uses more Sino-Korean (한자어) vocabulary than spoken Korean.

📧 Contoh kalimat untuk surel
BagianKoreaInggris
Pembuka 안녕하십니까, [이름] 님. Yth. [Nama],
Perkenalan diri 저는 [회사]의 [이름]입니다. Saya [Nama] dari [Perusahaan].
Tujuan 다름이 아니라 ~에 관하여 연락드립니다. Saya menulis surat ini sehubungan dengan ~.
Permintaan 검토해 주시면 감사하겠습니다. Saya akan sangat berterima kasih bila Anda berkenan meninjaunya.
Penutup 앞으로도 잘 부탁드립니다. Saya menantikan dukungan Anda selanjutnya.
Salam penutup 감사드립니다. [이름] 드림 Terima kasih. Hormat saya, [Nama]
💡
드림 vs 올림
Untuk menutup surel, gunakan 드림 (e.g., 김민수 드림) for general professional sign-offs. Use 올림 ketika menulis kepada orang yang jauh lebih senior — bentuk ini menyiratkan kerendahan hati yang lebih dalam. Keduanya berarti "dari", tetapi berbeda tingkat penghormatannya.
