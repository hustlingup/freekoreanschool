Coreano de negocios: 비즈니스 한국어

🏢 Expresiones para el entorno laboral 📊 Avanzado 🔖 Ámbito profesional 🤝 Etiqueta empresarial

La cultura empresarial coreana concede una gran importancia a la jerarquía, la formalidad y el respeto. Whether you are joining a Korean company, working with Korean partners, or attending a business meeting in Korea, mastering professional Korean (비즈니스 한국어) will set you apart. The formal speech level (격식체) is essential — never use casual speech (반말) in a business setting.

1 Formal Speech in Business (격식체)

Las empresas coreanas utilizan formal polite speech (격식체/합쇼체) — the most respectful register. This is more formal than everyday 존댓말 (해요체). Mastering these endings is non-negotiable in professional settings.

💡
El patrón esencial
En el registro formal, los verbos terminan en -습니다/-ㅂ니다 (enunciados) y -습니까/-ㅂ니까 (questions). Replace the -(아/어)요 ending you use in daily conversation. This instantly communicates professionalism.

2 Business Greetings (비즈니스 인사)

First impressions in Korean business settings are critical. A deep bow (고개를 숙이다) accompanies formal greetings. Learn these phrases to make a strong professional impression.

3 Business Card Exchange (명함 교환)

Exchanging business cards (명함) is a formal ritual in Korean business culture. Cards are presented and received with ambas manos, con una leve inclinación. Nunca escribas sobre una tarjeta ni te la guardes en el bolsillo trasero: se considera una falta de respeto.

4 Korean Business Hierarchy & Job Titles (직급)

Korean companies follow a strict hierarchy. Understanding titles (직급) is essential for addressing colleagues correctly — always use their title, not their first name.

💡
Tratamiento por el cargo
In Korea, you address colleagues by their title + 님: 부장님, 과장님. Usa el nombre de una persona solo si te invita expresamente a hacerlo. Llamar a alguien por su nombre de pila en un contexto profesional sin que te lo haya pedido se considera una falta de educación.

7 Email & Written Communication (이메일)

Korean business emails follow strict conventions. Always open with a respectful greeting, state your purpose clearly, and close with a polite sign-off. Written Korean uses more Sino-Korean (한자어) vocabulary than spoken Korean.

📧 Fórmulas para correos electrónicos
ParteCoreanoInglés
Apertura 안녕하십니까, [이름] 님. Estimado/a [Nombre]:
Presentación personal 저는 [회사]의 [이름]입니다. Soy [Nombre], de [Empresa].
Finalidad 다름이 아니라 ~에 관하여 연락드립니다. Le escribo en relación con ~.
Petición 검토해 주시면 감사하겠습니다. Le agradecería que pudiera revisarlo.
Cierre 앞으로도 잘 부탁드립니다. Espero seguir contando con su apoyo.
Despedida 감사드립니다. [이름] 드림 Muchas gracias. Atentamente, [Nombre]
💡
드림 vs 올림
Para despedirte en un correo, utiliza 드림 (e.g., 김민수 드림) for general professional sign-offs. Use 올림 cuando escribes a alguien de rango muy superior: expresa una humildad añadida. Ambas formas significan «de parte de», pero se diferencian en el grado de respeto.
