Shopping Korean: 쇼핑 표현

🛒 Market to Mall Phrases 📊 Intermediate 🔖 Practical Korean 💰 Bargaining Expressions

Master the language of Korean shopping — from browsing traditional markets to paying at department stores. These phrases will make your shopping experience in Korea confident and enjoyable.

Intermediate Practical

1 Entering a Store 가게 입장

The moment you walk into a Korean shop, you'll hear 어서오세요 — the warm welcome phrase every shopkeeper uses. Here are the essential phrases for those first moments.

KoreanRomanizationEnglish日本語Listen
어서오세요 Eoseoosei-yo オソオセヨ Welcome! (Come right in!)
뭘 찾으세요? Mwol chajeuseyo? ムォル チャジュセヨ What are you looking for?
그냥 둘러볼게요 Geunyang dulleobolgkeyo グニャン トゥルロポルッケヨ Just browsing
도움이 필요하세요? Doumi piryohaseyo? トウミ ピリョハセヨ Do you need help?
잠깐만요 Jamkkanmanyo チャムッカンマニョ Just a moment
죄송합니다 Joesonghamnida チェソンハムニダ Excuse me / Sorry

4 Prices and Numbers in Shopping 가격과 숫자

Korean currency is the 원 (won). Prices in Korea can seem large because the won has no cent equivalent — a coffee costs around 5,000–6,000원. Here are the key denominations to know.

ℹ️
Korean Won (원) Key Amounts
천원 (cheon won) = 1,000₩  |  오천원 (ocheon won) = 5,000₩  |  만원 (man won) = 10,000₩  |  오만원 (oman won) = 50,000₩

5 Traditional Market vs Department Store 시장 vs 백화점

Shopping in Korea means two very different worlds — the bustling traditional market where bargaining is expected, and the polished department store where prices are fixed. Here's what you need for the market.

ℹ️
Must-Visit Korean Markets
광장시장 (Gwangjang Market) in Seoul — famous for traditional Korean street food and vintage clothing. Great for practicing bargaining!

남대문시장 (Namdaemun Market) — one of Korea's largest traditional markets, open from early morning. Best for wholesale prices on clothing, accessories, and souvenirs. Vendors appreciate basic Korean — even a few phrases go a long way!
KoreanRomanizationEnglish日本語Listen
몇 개 사면 싸게 해줘요? myeot gae samyeon ssage haejwoyo? ミョッ ゲ サミョン サゲ ヘジュォヨ Cheaper if I buy more?
덤으로 주세요 deogeuro juseyo トグロ チュセヨ Please give me a little extra
봉투 주세요 bongtu juseyo ポントゥ チュセヨ Please give me a bag
포장해 주세요 pojang-hae juseyo ポジャンヘ チュセヨ Please wrap it (as a gift)
💡
Tip: Bargaining Culture
Bargaining is acceptable at traditional markets (시장) but NOT at department stores (백화점), convenience stores (편의점), or branded shops. At markets, always ask politely — being friendly and using Korean phrases greatly increases your chances of a discount!

7 Returns and Exchanges 반품 교환

Sometimes things don't work out — here's how to return or exchange items in Korean.

KoreanRomanizationEnglish日本語Listen
반품할게요 banpum-halgeyo パンプム ハルゲヨ I want to return this
교환할게요 gyohwan-halgeyo キョファン ハルゲヨ I want to exchange this
영수증 있어요 yeongsujeung isseoyo ヨンスジュン イッソヨ I have the receipt
불량품이에요 bullyangpum-ieyo プルリャンプム イエヨ It's defective
사이즈가 안 맞아요 saijeuga an majayo サイジュガ アン マジャヨ The size doesn't fit
환불 돼요? hwanbul dwaeyo? ファンブル テヨ Can I get a refund?
💡
Tip: Return Policies in Korea
Most stores allow returns within 7–14 days with the original receipt and tags attached. Department stores generally have more lenient return policies than small independent shops. Always ask 환불 돼요? before purchasing if you're unsure about sizing.

South Korea has one of the world's most advanced e-commerce systems. Whether you're using Coupang, Naver Shopping, or 11번가, these terms are essential.

💡
Tip: Major Korean Online Shopping Platforms
쿠팡 (Coupang) — Korea's Amazon equivalent; famous for "로켓배송" (Rocket Delivery) — next-day or same-day shipping.
네이버 쇼핑 (Naver Shopping) — aggregates sellers; integrated with Naver Pay (네이버페이).
11번가 — Large marketplace with regular sales events.
무신사 (Musinsa) — Korea's top fashion e-commerce platform for streetwear and K-fashion.

9 Complete Shopping Dialogue 종합 대화

Put it all together! Here's a full shopping conversation at a Korean clothing store — from greeting to goodbye.

💬 At a Clothing Store — Full Dialogue
직원: 어서오세요! 뭘 찾으세요?
Staff: Eoseoosei-yo! Mwol chajeuseyo?
Staff: Welcome! What are you looking for?
店員：いらっしゃいませ！何かお探しですか？
손님: 그냥 둘러볼게요. 아, 이 재킷 예쁘다! 얼마예요?
Customer: Geunyang dulleobolgkeyo. A, i jaekit yeppeuda! Eolmayeyo?
Customer: Just browsing. Oh, this jacket is pretty! How much is it?
お客様：ただ見ているだけです。あ、このジャケットきれい！いくらですか？
직원: 육만 오천 원이에요.
Staff: Yungman ocheon won-ieyo.
Staff: It's 65,000 won.
店員：6万5千ウォンです。
손님: 다른 색깔 있어요?
Customer: Dareun saekkal isseoyo?
Customer: Do you have other colors?
お客様：他の色はありますか？
직원: 네, 검은색이랑 흰색 있어요.
Staff: Ne, geomeunsaeki-rang huinsaek isseoyo.
Staff: Yes, we have black and white.
店員：はい、黒と白がございます。
손님: 검은색으로 입어봐도 돼요?
Customer: Geomeunsaeg-euro ibeobwado dwaeyo?
Customer: Can I try on the black one?
お客様：黒いのを試着してもいいですか？
직원: 물론이죠! 탈의실은 저쪽이에요.
Staff: Mullon-ijyo! Tali-sireun jeojjok-ieyo.
Staff: Of course! The fitting room is over there.
店員：もちろんです！試着室はあちらです。
손님: 딱 맞아요! 이걸로 살게요. 카드 돼요?
Customer: Ttak majayo! Igeol-lo salgeyo. Kadeu dwaeyo?
Customer: It fits perfectly! I'll take this one. Can I pay by card?
お客様：ぴったりです！これにします。カードで払えますか？
직원: 네, 카드 됩니다! 여기 영수증이에요.
Staff: Ne, kadeu dwemnida! Yeogi yeongsujeung-ieyo.
Staff: Yes, card is fine! Here is your receipt.
店員：はい、カードでOKです！こちらが領収書です。
손님: 감사합니다! 안녕히 계세요.
Customer: Gamsahamnida! Annyeonghi gyeseyo.
Customer: Thank you! Goodbye (to someone staying).
お客様：ありがとうございます！また来ます。
