Stage 1 · How Strokes Work 획순 원리

The Three Rules of Stroke Order 획순 세 가지 규칙

Every hangul letter is built from a handful of simple strokes, and those strokes always come in the same order. Three rules cover all of them.

  • Top before bottom — the highest stroke is written first.
  • Left before right — when strokes stand side by side, start with the one on the left.
  • Horizontal before an intersecting vertical — draw the crossing bar first, then the line that cuts through it.

✍️ Why it matters

Every hangul letter follows these three rules. Learn the 24 basic shapes and you can write all 11,172 Korean syllables.

One Letter, One Rhythm 글자마다 정해진 획수

Each letter has a fixed number of strokes, and each stroke has a fixed direction. ㅈ is written in 2 strokes and ㅊ in 3 — that is the handwriting convention used all the way through this lesson. Inside a syllable block the order is fixed too: first the opening consonant, then the vowel, then the final consonant underneath.

  • Opening consonant first (초성) — the top-left slot.
  • Then the vowel (중성) — to the right of it, or underneath it.
  • Then the final consonant (종성) along the bottom, if the syllable has one.

💡 The mark on top of ㅎ and ㅊ

The small mark on top of ㅎ and ㅊ can be written flat, slanted, or straight up and down — Korean has no official rule, and you will see all three. We draw it slanted, the way most Korean fonts print it, so the letter you practise looks like the Korean you will actually read.

How to write each letter

LetterStrokesStroke orderExample
ㄱ g / k1One single stroke: across to the right, then turn and come straight down.가다 — to go
ㄴ n1One single stroke: down the left side, then turn and run along the bottom.나 — I / me
ㄷ d / t2Two strokes: the top bar, then a stroke down the left side and along the bottom.다 — all / every
ㄹ r / l3Three strokes: the ㄱ motion, the middle bar, then the ㄴ motion.리 — reason (prefix)
ㅁ m3Three strokes: the left side down, then the top and right side in one motion, then the bottom bar.마음 — heart / mind
ㅂ b / p4Four strokes: the left post down, the right post down, then the middle bar, then the bottom bar.밥 — rice / meal
ㅅ s2Two strokes: the long left leg first, then the shorter right leg.사람 — person
ㅇ ng / silent1One single stroke: a closed circle, starting at the top and coming back to the same point.아이 — child
ㅈ j2Two strokes: the top bar and the left leg in one motion, then the right leg. Two, not three.지금 — now
ㅊ ch3Three strokes: the small slanted mark on top, then the bar with its left leg, then the right leg.차 — tea / car
ㅋ k2Two strokes: the ㄱ shape first, then the short bar across the middle.커피 — coffee
ㅌ t3Three strokes: the top bar, the middle bar, then the left side and bottom in one motion.타다 — to ride
ㅍ p4Four strokes: the top bar, the left post, the right post, then the bottom bar.파 — green onion
ㅎ h3Three strokes: the small slanted mark, the bar under it, then the circle.하늘 — sky
