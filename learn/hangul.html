Learn Hangul in 1 Hour: 한글

🎯 6 Stages · 84 Steps 📊 Beginner ⏱️ ~60–90 min 🔥 Daily streak + real progress tracking
  • Created in 1443 by King Sejong the Great
  • Phonetic alphabet — each symbol = one sound
  • 6 stages to cover every character
  • Most learners finish in under 90 minutes

Stage 1 · Consonants 기본 자음

Letters and sounds

LetterPronunciationExampleMeaningNote
ㄱg / k가다to goShaped like the back of the throat blocking airflow.
ㄴn나I / meShaped like a tongue tip touching the roof of the mouth.
ㄷd / t다all / everyLike ㄴ with a roof — tongue fully blocks the palate.
ㄹr / l리reason (prefix)A flap — between English 'r' and 'l'. Tongue taps the palate.
ㅁm마음heart / mindShaped like closed lips pressing together to make 'm'.
ㅂb / p밥rice / mealShaped like lips opening to release the 'b' or 'p' sound.
ㅅs사람personShaped like two teeth — air hisses through for the 's' sound.
ㅇng / silent아이childSilent at the start of a syllable. Sounds like 'ng' at the end.
ㅈj지금nowLike 'j' in 'juice'. Tongue presses behind upper teeth.
ㅊch차tea / carAspirated 'ch' — a puff of air comes out. Like 'ch' in 'cheese'.
ㅋk커피coffeeAspirated 'k' — strong puff of air. Hold a tissue and it flutters.
ㅌt타다to rideAspirated 't' — like 't' in 'ten' (at word start). Strong burst.
ㅍp파green onionAspirated 'p' — like 'p' in 'pot'. Lips pop apart with air.
ㅎh하늘skyA breathy 'h' — like exhaling warmth onto your hand.

Check your understanding

ㄱ romanizes as…
Answer: g/k (n · g/k · d/t · m)
Which consonant is SILENT at the start of a syllable?
Answer: ㅇ (ㄱ · ㄴ · ㅇ · ㅅ)
ㄴ romanizes as…
Answer: n (r/l · m · n · b/p)
Which consonant sounds like 'j' in 'juice'?
Answer: ㅈ (ㅂ · ㅈ · ㅅ · ㄷ)
ㅁ romanizes as…
Answer: m (b · n · m · h)
ㄹ romanizes as…
Answer: r/l (n · d/t · r/l · s)
밥 (rice) starts with which consonant?
Answer: ㅂ (ㅂ · ㅍ · ㅁ · ㄹ)

Stage 2 · Vowels 기본 모음

Letters and sounds

LetterPronunciationExampleMeaningNote
아 ㅏaOpen your mouth wide, like saying 'ah' at the doctor.
야 ㅑyaLike 'ya' in 'yard'. The extra stroke means a 'y' is added.
어 ㅓeoSimilar to 'uh' or 'o' in 'the other'. Lips slightly rounded.
여 ㅕyeoLike 'yuh' with rounded lips. Two strokes = 'y' prefix.
오 ㅗoLike 'o' in 'more'. Round your lips as if blowing a candle.
요 ㅛyoLike 'yo!' — two strokes add the 'y'. Used a lot in speech.
우 ㅜuLike 'oo' in 'boot'. The line points DOWN — mouth drops.
유 ㅠyuLike 'you'. Two downward strokes signal the 'y' prefix.
으 ㅡeuLike the 'e' sound when you're unsatisfied: 'euugh'. Flat lips.
이 ㅣiLike 'ee' in 'bee'. A simple vertical stroke — pure high front vowel.

Check your understanding

Which vowel sounds like 'a' (as in 'father')?
Answer: ㅏ (ㅏ · ㅗ · ㅜ · ㅣ)
Which vowel sounds like 'o' (as in 'more')?
Answer: ㅗ (ㅓ · ㅗ · ㅡ · ㅑ)
Which vowel sounds like 'ee' (as in 'bee')?
Answer: ㅣ (ㅓ · ㅛ · ㅣ · ㅠ)
ㅠ romanizes as…
Answer: yu (u · eu · yo · yu)
ㅡ sounds like…
Answer: eu (ya · i · eu · yo)

Stage 4 · Special 쌍자음 · 거센소리

Letters and sounds

LetterPronunciationExampleMeaningNote
ㄲkkTense 'k' — hold your breath then release. No air puff.
ㄸttTense 't' — throat tightens before releasing. Distinct from ㄷ and ㅌ.
ㅃppTense 'p' — lips press hard before popping open. No air escapes.
ㅆssTense 's' — a sharp, tight hiss. Used in 있다 (to exist / to have).
ㅉjjTense 'j' — sharper and more abrupt than ㅈ. No air burst.
ㅋk (aspirated)Aspirated — a strong burst of air follows the consonant.
ㅌt (aspirated)Aspirated — tongue snaps off the palate with a puff of air.
ㅍp (aspirated)Aspirated — lips burst open with a pop of air. Like 'pot'.
ㅊch (aspirated)Aspirated 'ch' — like 'cheese' at the start. Air comes out.
ㅎh (breathy)A breathy aspirated consonant. The most air of any Korean consonant.

Check your understanding

ㄲ romanizes as…
Answer: kk (k · kk · g/k · ng)
Which is a TENSE consonant (쌍자음)?
Answer: ㅆ (ㅋ · ㅌ · ㅆ · ㅊ)
ㅃ romanizes as…
Answer: pp (b · p · pp · bb)
Aspirated consonants are produced with…
Answer: A strong puff of air (No air · A strong puff of air · A tense throat · A nasal sound)
ㅉ romanizes as…
Answer: jj (ss · ch · jj · tt)

Stage 5 · Compound Vowels 복합 모음

Letters and sounds

LetterPronunciationExampleMeaningNote
애 ㅐaeㅏ + ㅣ combined. Like 'e' in 'bed'. Modern Korean = same as 에.
에 ㅔeㅓ + ㅣ combined. Like 'e' in 'bed'. Sounds the same as 애 today.
얘 ㅒyaeㅑ + ㅣ. Rare — appears in 얘 (this child, colloquial).
예 ㅖyeㅕ + ㅣ. Like 'ye' in 'yes'. 예쁘다 = beautiful.
와 ㅘwaㅗ + ㅏ. Like 'wa' in 'water'. 와! = Wow!
왜 ㅙwaeㅗ + ㅐ. Sounds like 'wae'. 왜 = why.
외 ㅚoeㅗ + ㅣ. Like 'we'. 외국인 = foreigner.
워 ㅝwoㅜ + ㅓ. Like 'wuh'. 뭐 = what (colloquial).
웨 ㅞweㅜ + ㅔ. Like 'we'. Very rare in Korean.
위 ㅟwiㅜ + ㅣ. Like 'wee'. 위 = above / stomach.
의 ㅢuiㅡ + ㅣ. A unique glide. 의사 = doctor. Used as possessive particle 의.

Check your understanding

ㅘ romanizes as…
Answer: wa (wo · wa · wae · oe)
왜 means…
Answer: why (what · who · why · where)
ㅢ romanizes as…
Answer: ui (wi · ui · we · eu)
Which compound vowel uses ㅗ + ㅣ?
Answer: ㅚ (ㅘ · ㅙ · ㅚ · ㅝ)
예 romanizes as…
Answer: ye (yae · ye · ae · e)
