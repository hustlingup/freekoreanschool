Korean Typing ⌨️

Korea types on the Dubeolsik (두벌식) "2-set" layout: consonants on the left half of the keyboard, vowels on the right. Learn where each letter lives, then practise until the blocks build themselves.

🎯 6 Stages · 29 Steps 📊 Beginner ⏱️ ~38 min 🔥 Daily streak + real progress tracking
  • The whole 두벌식 (Dubeolsik) 2-set layout, key by key — consonants on the left half, vowels on the right, tense consonants on Shift
  • How letters assemble into blocks as you type: ㅎ + ㅏ + ㄴ becomes 한, and a final consonant moves to the next block when a vowel follows
  • Drills at three levels — single jamo, then syllables with and without batchim, then real words and phrases
  • Every drill scores you in 타/분 (characters per minute) with live accuracy, ending in a 60-second speed sprint
  • No Korean keyboard to install: physical keys and the on-screen keyboard both feed the lesson's own composer
  • Finishable entirely on a phone — tap the on-screen keys — with a final step showing how to enable Korean input on iOS, Android, Windows and macOS

Use your own keyboard or click the on-screen keys — either way the letters combine into real syllable blocks. Hold Shift for the tense consonants, and watch your speed in 타/분 (characters per minute) as you go.

お使いのキーボードでも、画面上のキーをクリックしても構いません。入力した字母はそのまま音節ブロックに合成されます。濃音はShiftキー、速度は타/분（1分あたりの打鍵数）で表示されます。

可以使用實體鍵盤，也可以點選畫面上的按鍵，字母都會自動組合成音節積木。按住 Shift 可輸入緊音，並可即時查看 타/분（每分鐘字數）速度。
