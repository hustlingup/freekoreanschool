1 · Vowel Recognition

Full lesson →Back to topics ↑NIKL romanization reference →
1. What sound does ㅏ make?

Answer

a

ㅏ · Open your mouth wide, like saying 'ah' at the doctor.

ㅏ is romanized a, as in 아. Compare its right-facing short stroke with ㅓ; changing that direction changes the vowel.

Full lesson →
2. What sound does ㅓ make?

Answer

eo

ㅓ · Similar to 'uh' or 'o' in 'the other'. Lips slightly rounded.

ㅓ is romanized eo, a single vowel rather than an English e followed by o. Compare 어 and 오 using audio; romanization is a reading aid.

Full lesson →
3. What sound does ㅗ make?

Answer

o

ㅗ · Like 'o' in 'more'. Round your lips as if blowing a candle.

ㅗ is romanized o. Its short stroke points upward. Add the silent initial ㅇ to write the vowel syllable 오.

Full lesson →
4. What sound does ㅜ make?

Answer

u

ㅜ · Like 'oo' in 'boot'. The line points DOWN — mouth drops.

ㅜ is romanized u. Its short stroke points downward, unlike ㅗ. Compare 우 and 오 rather than relying only on English spelling.

Full lesson →
5. What sound does ㅡ make?

Answer

eu

ㅡ · Like the 'e' sound when you're unsatisfied: 'euugh'. Flat lips.

ㅡ is romanized eu and written as a horizontal line. Eu represents one Korean vowel, not two syllables.

Full lesson →
6. What sound does ㅣ make?

Answer

i

ㅣ · Like 'ee' in 'bee'. A simple vertical stroke — pure high front vowel.

ㅣ is romanized i and written as a vertical line. The syllable 이 adds an initial ㅇ, which is silent in that position.

Full lesson →
7. What sound does ㅑ make?

Answer

ya

ㅑ · Like 'ya' in 'yard'. The extra stroke means a 'y' is added.

ㅑ adds a y sound to ㅏ. The extra short stroke helps distinguish 야 from 아.

Full lesson →
8. What sound does ㅕ make?

Answer

yeo

ㅕ · Like 'yuh' with rounded lips. Two strokes = 'y' prefix.

ㅕ adds a y sound to ㅓ. Compare 여 and 어; do not replace eo with the separate vowel o.

Full lesson →
9. What sound does ㅛ make?

Answer

yo

ㅛ · Like 'yo!' — two strokes add the 'y'. Used a lot in speech.

ㅛ is the y counterpart of ㅗ. Compare 요 and 오, and notice the two upward strokes.

Full lesson →
10. What sound does ㅠ make?

Answer

yu

ㅠ · Like 'you'. Two downward strokes signal the 'y' prefix.

ㅠ is the y counterpart of ㅜ. Compare 유 and 우; both short strokes point downward.

Full lesson →
11. Which vowel makes the sound "a"?

Answer

ㅏ

ㅏ · Open your mouth wide, like saying 'ah' at the doctor.

ㅏ is the letter for a. In a complete syllable, 아 combines silent initial ㅇ with ㅏ.

Full lesson →
12. Which vowel makes the sound "eo"?

Answer

ㅓ

ㅓ · Similar to 'uh' or 'o' in 'the other'. Lips slightly rounded.

ㅓ represents eo. Its short stroke points left; ㅏ points right. Practice distinguishing 어 and 아.

Full lesson →
13. What sound does ㅐ make?

Answer

ae

ㅐ · ㅏ + ㅣ combined. Like 'e' in 'bed'. Modern Korean = same as 에.

ㅐ is romanized ae. Its pronunciation often overlaps with ㅔ in contemporary speech, so a spelling distinction is not always an audible contrast.

Full lesson →
14. What sound does ㅔ make?

Answer

e

ㅔ · ㅓ + ㅣ combined. Like 'e' in 'bed'. Sounds the same as 애 today.

ㅔ is romanized e. Many speakers do not consistently distinguish it from ㅐ in speech; learn the spellings in words as well as listening.

Full lesson →
15. What sound does ㅘ make?

Answer

wa

ㅘ · ㅗ + ㅏ. Like 'wa' in 'water'. 와! = Wow!

ㅘ combines ㅗ and ㅏ and is romanized wa. 와 is one syllable, not 오 followed by 아 as two separate syllables.

Full lesson →
16. What sound does ㅝ make?

Answer

weo / wo

ㅝ · ㅜ + ㅓ. Like 'wuh'. 뭐 = what (colloquial).

ㅝ combines ㅜ and ㅓ. Standard Revised Romanization uses wo; weo in this quiz is an explanatory spelling that shows the eo component.

Full lesson →
17. What sound does ㅟ make?

Answer

wi

ㅟ · ㅜ + ㅣ. Like 'wee'. 위 = above / stomach.

ㅟ combines ㅜ and ㅣ and is romanized wi. Listen to 위 as a whole syllable rather than reading two English vowel letters separately.

Full lesson →
18. What sound does ㅢ make?

Answer

ui

ㅢ · ㅡ + ㅣ. A unique glide. 의사 = doctor. Used as possessive particle 의.

ㅢ is romanized ui. Its pronunciation changes with position and grammatical use; the possessive particle 의 can be pronounced 에.

Full lesson →
19. Which vowel is formed by combining ㅗ + ㅏ?

Answer

ㅘ

ㅘ · ㅗ + ㅏ. Like 'wa' in 'water'. 와! = Wow!

ㅗ + ㅏ forms ㅘ. Put initial ㅇ before the combined vowel to make 와, a single syllable block.

Full lesson →
20. Which vowel is formed by combining ㅜ + ㅣ?

Answer

ㅟ

ㅟ · ㅜ + ㅣ. Like 'wee'. 위 = above / stomach.

ㅜ + ㅣ forms ㅟ. Compare 위 with 우 to notice the different vowel; both need an initial consonant position in the block.

2 · Consonant Recognition

Full lesson →Back to topics ↑NIKL romanization reference →
1. What sound does ㄱ make?

Answer

g / k

ㄱ · Shaped like the back of the throat blocking airflow.

ㄱ is a plain consonant represented by g or k depending on position and sound context. Compare it with tense ㄲ and aspirated ㅋ using audio.

Full lesson →
2. What sound does ㄴ make?

Answer

n

ㄴ · Shaped like a tongue tip touching the roof of the mouth.

ㄴ represents n. In 나 it starts the syllable; in 산 it occupies the final-consonant position.

Full lesson →
3. What sound does ㄷ make?

Answer

d / t

ㄷ · Like ㄴ with a roof — tongue fully blocks the palate.

ㄷ is a plain consonant represented by d or t depending on context. A final ㄷ is not pronounced as a separately released English d.

Full lesson →
4. What sound does ㄹ make?

Answer

r / l

ㄹ · A flap — between English 'r' and 'l'. Tongue taps the palate.

ㄹ is represented by r or l depending on position. A single ㄹ between vowels commonly has a brief tap; final ㄹ is l-like.

Full lesson →
5. What sound does ㅁ make?

Answer

m

ㅁ · Shaped like closed lips pressing together to make 'm'.

ㅁ represents m. In 마 it starts the block; in 감 it closes the syllable. Identify its position before reading the whole word.

Full lesson →
6. What sound does ㅂ make?

Answer

b / p

ㅂ · Shaped like lips opening to release the 'b' or 'p' sound.

ㅂ is a plain consonant represented by b or p depending on context. Contrast 바, 빠, and 파 rather than treating b and p as exact English equivalents.

Full lesson →
7. What sound does ㅅ make?

Answer

s

ㅅ · Shaped like two teeth — air hisses through for the 's' sound.

ㅅ is generally represented by s initially. Its pronunciation changes with the following vowel and in final position, so the isolated label does not cover every word.

Full lesson →
8. What sound does ㅇ make at the START of a syllable?

Answer

silent

ㅇ · Silent at the start of a syllable. Sounds like 'ng' at the end.

Initial ㅇ fills the required consonant position without adding a consonant sound: 아 begins with the vowel a.

Full lesson →
9. What sound does ㅇ make at the END of a syllable?

Answer

ng

ㅇ · Silent at the start of a syllable. Sounds like 'ng' at the end.

Final ㅇ represents ng, as in 강. Unlike initial ㅇ, it contributes an audible final consonant.

Full lesson →
10. What sound does ㅈ make?

Answer

j

ㅈ · Like 'j' in 'juice'. Tongue presses behind upper teeth.

ㅈ is represented by j initially in the basic table. Compare 자, 짜, and 차 for the plain, tense, and aspirated contrast.

Full lesson →
11. Which is the tensed (double) version of ㄱ?

Answer

ㄲ

ㄲ · Tense 'k' — hold your breath then release. No air puff.

ㄲ is the tense counterpart of ㄱ. The doubled spelling represents a tense consonant, not two separately spoken ㄱ sounds.

Full lesson →
12. Which is the tensed (double) version of ㄷ?

Answer

ㄸ

ㄸ · Tense 't' — throat tightens before releasing. Distinct from ㄷ and ㅌ.

ㄸ is the tense counterpart of ㄷ. Practice 다 and 따 as different consonants rather than merely saying one more loudly.

Full lesson →
13. Which is the tensed (double) version of ㅂ?

Answer

ㅃ

ㅃ · Tense 'p' — lips press hard before popping open. No air escapes.

ㅃ is the tense counterpart of ㅂ. Compare 바 and 빠; the doubled letter is not a sequence of two syllables.

Full lesson →
14. Which is the aspirated version of ㄱ?

Answer

ㅋ

ㅋ · Aspirated 'k' — strong puff of air. Hold a tissue and it flutters.

ㅋ is the aspirated counterpart of ㄱ. The stronger puff of air distinguishes it from the plain and tense series.

Full lesson →
15. Which is the aspirated version of ㄷ?

Answer

ㅌ

ㅌ · Aspirated 't' — like 't' in 'ten' (at word start). Strong burst.

ㅌ is the aspirated counterpart of ㄷ. Compare 다, 따, and 타 with audio, paying attention to the consonant rather than the vowel.

Full lesson →
16. Which is the aspirated version of ㅂ?

Answer

ㅍ

ㅍ · Aspirated 'p' — like 'p' in 'pot'. Lips pop apart with air.

ㅍ is the aspirated counterpart of ㅂ. Compare 바, 빠, and 파 to hear the three-way contrast.

Full lesson →
17. What sound does ㄲ make?

Answer

kk (tense)

ㄲ · Tense 'k' — hold your breath then release. No air puff.

ㄲ is commonly romanized kk. That spelling labels a tense consonant; pronouncing two English k sounds separately will not reproduce it.

Full lesson →
18. What sound does ㅋ make?

Answer

k (aspirated)

ㅋ · Aspirated 'k' — strong puff of air. Hold a tissue and it flutters.

ㅋ is an aspirated consonant. Its contrast with ㄱ and ㄲ matters even when all three seem k-like to an English-speaking learner.

Full lesson →
19. What sound does ㅎ make?

Answer

h

ㅎ · A breathy 'h' — like exhaling warmth onto your hand.

ㅎ represents h in the basic table. In connected speech it can also interact with adjacent consonants, so learn sound changes in complete words.

Full lesson →
20. The compound batchim ㄳ is made of which two consonants?

Answer

ㄱ + ㅅ

ㄳ combines ㄱ and ㅅ in the final-consonant position. The written components do not guarantee that both will be separately pronounced in every context.

1. 안녕하세요 is read as?

Answer

Annyeonghaseyo

Read 안녕하세요 in five blocks: 안 · 녕 · 하 · 세 · 요. The final 요 makes this a polite everyday greeting.

Full lesson →
2. Which word means "love"?

Answer

사랑

사랑 means love. 사랑해요 adds a polite verb ending to say “I love you”; the noun alone is not the complete sentence.

Full lesson →
3. 감사합니다 is read as?

Answer

Gamsahamnida

감사합니다 means “Thank you.” Its spelling contains 합, but the ㅂ before ㄴ is pronounced as ㅁ: 함니다 in the spoken sequence.

Full lesson →
4. Which word means "school"?

Answer

학교

학교 means school. Read 학 · 교. The written ㄱ + ㄱ sequence is pronounced with a tense consonant in this word.

Full lesson →
5. What does 물 mean?

Answer

Water

물 means water. 물 주세요 asks for water. Notice that the final ㄹ belongs to the same block as ㅁ and ㅜ.

Full lesson →
6. What does 밥 mean?

Answer

Rice / Meal

밥 can mean cooked rice or a meal. 밥 먹었어요? commonly asks whether someone has eaten; context determines the best translation.

Full lesson →
7. Which word means "person / people"?

Answer

사람

사람 means a person or people, with number inferred from context. 사람들 explicitly adds the plural suffix 들 when that distinction is useful.

Full lesson →
8. 한국 is the Korean word for?

Answer

Korea

한국 names Korea. 한국어 names the Korean language, so the country name and the language name are related but not interchangeable.

Full lesson →
9. What does 집 mean?

Answer

House / Home

집 means a house or home. 집에 가요 means “I go home,” with 에 marking the destination.

Full lesson →
10. Which word means "friend"?

Answer

친구

친구 means friend. 같은 나이의 친구 describes a friend of the same age, but the bare dictionary meaning does not establish a relationship by itself.

Full lesson →
11. What does 하늘 mean?

Answer

Sky

하늘 means sky. In 하늘이 맑아요, “The sky is clear,” 이 follows 하늘 because it ends in a consonant.

Full lesson →
12. Which word means "sea / ocean"?

Answer

바다

바다 means sea or ocean. 바다에 가요 says you are going to the sea; the destination particle follows the whole noun.

Full lesson →
13. 화이팅 / 파이팅 is an expression meaning?

Answer

You can do it! / Go for it!

파이팅 or 화이팅 is a cheer of encouragement. It does not ordinarily instruct someone to physically fight.

Full lesson →
14. What does 맛있어요 mean?

Answer

It's delicious

맛있어요 means something tastes good or is delicious. The subject can be omitted when the food being discussed is clear.

Full lesson →
15. Which word means "heart / mind"?

Answer

마음

마음 refers to the mind, heart, or feelings in different contexts. It is not the anatomical word for the heart, 심장.

Full lesson →

받침 is a consonant written in the final position of a syllable block. In 강, ㅇ is the 받침; in 가, there is no 받침.

Full lesson →
17. What does 영어 mean?

English language

영어 names the English language. 영어를 공부해요 means “I study English,” with 를 marking what is studied.

Full lesson →
18. Which word means "tree"?

나무

나무 means tree or wood depending on context. 나무 한 그루 counts one tree; the counter helps identify the intended unit.

카메라 is the Korean loanword for camera. It is written in three blocks: 카 · 메 · 라.

가방 means bag. 가방이 있어요 means “There is a bag / I have a bag,” with the owner understood from context.

4 · Greetings & Phrases

Full lesson →Back to topics ↑
1. How do you say "Hello" formally?

Answer

안녕하세요

안녕하세요 is polite everyday speech. The question uses “formally” broadly; the more formal greeting 안녕하십니까 belongs to a different speech style.

Full lesson →
2. What does 감사합니다 mean?

Answer

Thank you (formal)

감사합니다 expresses thanks with the formal polite ending -ㅂ니다. 고마워요 is another polite expression in the 해요 style.

Full lesson →
3. How do you say "Nice to meet you"?

Answer

반갑습니다

반갑습니다 means “It is nice to meet you” in formal polite speech. 반가워요 gives a common polite everyday alternative.

Full lesson →
4. What does 죄송합니다 mean?

Answer

I'm very sorry (formal)

죄송합니다 is a formal polite apology. Choose the expression for the situation; it does not automatically measure how serious an incident was.

Full lesson →
5. What does 괜찮아요 mean?

Answer

It's okay / I'm fine

괜찮아요 can mean “It is okay” or “I am fine.” What is okay depends on the preceding question or situation.

Full lesson →
6. How do you say "Long time no see"?

Answer

오랜만이에요

오랜만이에요 means it has been a long time. Use it when meeting or contacting someone again after a gap.

Full lesson →
7. 어서 오세요 is said when...?

Answer

Welcoming someone to your store or home

어서 오세요 welcomes someone arriving. It is addressed to the arriving person, unlike a farewell such as 안녕히 가세요.

Full lesson →
8. What does 잘 먹겠습니다 mean?

Answer

I will eat well (said before meals)

잘 먹겠습니다 is a conventional expression before a meal. Its timing differs from 잘 먹었습니다, which follows the meal.

Full lesson →
9. What does 잘 먹었습니다 mean?

Answer

I ate well (said after meals)

잘 먹었습니다 expresses appreciation after eating. The past ending -었- helps distinguish it from the before-meal phrase.

Full lesson →
10. How do you say "See you again"?

Answer

또 봐요

또 봐요 means “See you again.” 또 means again; 봐요 is the polite everyday form of 보다 in this expression.

Full lesson →
11. 안녕히 가세요 is said to...?

Answer

The person who is leaving

안녕히 가세요 is directed to someone leaving. Think about the listener’s movement, not only whether you are leaving too.

Full lesson →
12. 안녕히 계세요 is said when...?

Answer

You yourself are the one leaving

안녕히 계세요 addresses someone who stays while you leave. The question’s answer assumes the listener remains; if both leave, 가세요 can fit.

Full lesson →
13. What does 수고하세요 mean?

Answer

Good work / Take care

수고하세요 acknowledges someone continuing their work. It is not a universal farewell; its appropriateness depends on relationship and setting.

Full lesson →
14. 천만에요 means?

Answer

You're welcome

천만에요 is taught as “You are welcome.” It is not the only natural response to thanks; 아니에요 is also common in many situations.

Full lesson →
15. How do you say "How have you been?"?

Answer

잘 지냈어요?

잘 지냈어요? asks how someone has been. 지냈어요 is a past form, so the question concerns the period since you last met.

Full lesson →
16. 처음 뵙겠습니다 is a formal phrase used when?

Answer

Meeting someone for the very first time

처음 뵙겠습니다 is a formal first-meeting expression. 뵙다 is a humble form associated with meeting or seeing someone respectfully.

Full lesson →
17. What does 잘 부탁드립니다 mean?

Answer

Please take care of me / Best regards

잘 부탁드립니다 asks for the other person’s goodwill or cooperation. Its English translation changes with the situation rather than literally always meaning personal care.

Full lesson →
18. 고마워요 is which speech level?

Answer

Polite informal (해요체)

고마워요 uses the polite informal 해요 style. “Informal” here describes the style; the ending 요 still makes it polite.

Full lesson →
19. What does 미안해요 mean?

Answer

Sorry (polite)

미안해요 is a polite everyday apology. 미안해 removes 요 and belongs to casual speech, so relationship matters.

Full lesson →
20. 네 / 아니요 means?

Answer

Yes / No

네 and 아니요 are basic polite responses meaning yes and no. Answers to negative questions need attention to the proposition being confirmed.

Answer

일

일 is Sino-Korean one. 하나 is native Korean one; 일 원 uses the Sino-Korean system for a money amount.

Full lesson →
2. What is the native Korean word for "two"?

Answer

둘

둘 is native Korean two. Before many counters it shortens to 두, as in 커피 두 잔, two cups of coffee.

Full lesson →
3. What is the Sino-Korean word for "ten"?

Answer

십

십 is Sino-Korean ten. 십 원 means ten won, whereas 열 is the native Korean word for ten.

Full lesson →
4. What is the native Korean word for "three"?

Answer

셋

셋 is native Korean three. Before a counter it commonly becomes 세: 책 세 권, three books.

Full lesson →
5. What is the Sino-Korean word for "hundred"?

Answer

백

백 is Sino-Korean one hundred. 이백 means two hundred; the system combines number words around units such as 백.

Full lesson →
6. When counting hours on a clock, which number system is used?

Answer

Native Korean (하나, 둘...)

Clock hours use native numbers: 한 시, 두 시, 세 시. Minutes use Sino-Korean numbers, so 2:15 is 두 시 십오 분.

Full lesson →
7. When talking about money (price), which number system is used?

Answer

Sino-Korean (일, 이...)

Money amounts use Sino-Korean numbers: 삼천 원 is three thousand won. Practice prices are examples, not current market information.

Full lesson →
8. How does a MALE speaker say "older brother"?

Answer

형

In the basic sibling relationship taught here, a male speaker calls an older brother 형. Social use beyond actual siblings depends on the relationship.

Full lesson →
9. How does a FEMALE speaker say "older brother"?

Answer

오빠

In the basic sibling relationship taught here, a female speaker calls an older brother 오빠. It does not automatically mean boyfriend.

Full lesson →
10. How does a FEMALE speaker say "older sister"?

Answer

언니

In the basic sibling relationship taught here, a female speaker calls an older sister 언니. Do not use it for every older female stranger.

Full lesson →
11. How does a MALE speaker say "older sister"?

Answer

누나

In the basic sibling relationship taught here, a male speaker calls an older sister 누나. These kinship terms depend on the speaker as well as the person addressed.

Full lesson →
12. What is the Korean word for "grandmother"?

Answer

할머니

할머니 means grandmother and can also address an older woman in some settings. The word alone does not make that address suitable for every stranger.

Full lesson →
13. What does 남동생 mean?

Answer

Younger brother

남동생 means younger brother. 동생 is younger sibling; 남 specifies male in this word.

Full lesson →
14. What does 여동생 mean?

Answer

Younger sister

여동생 means younger sister. 동생 identifies the younger sibling relationship; 여 specifies female.

Full lesson →
15. What is the Sino-Korean number for "four"?

Answer

사

사 is Sino-Korean four. 넷 is native Korean four and becomes 네 before many counters.

Full lesson →
16. What is the native Korean word for "ten"?

Answer

열

열 is native Korean ten. Compare 열 명, ten people, with 십 분, ten minutes.

Full lesson →
17. What does 아버지 / 아빠 mean?

Answer

Father / Dad

아버지 means father; 아빠 is the familiar “Dad.” Their register differs even though they refer to the same family role.

Full lesson →

Answer

Aunt (mother's sister)

이모 names your mother’s sister. 고모 names your father’s sister, so English “aunt” covers distinctions Korean makes.

Full lesson →

Answer

Uncle (father's brother)

삼촌 can refer to an uncle, including an unmarried paternal uncle, and has broader familiar uses. The quiz’s “father’s brother” gloss is a simplified example, not its complete range.

Answer

만

만 is Sino-Korean ten thousand. Korean groups large numbers around units such as 만: 이만 is twenty thousand.

가
