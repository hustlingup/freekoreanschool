About Korean School

A free Korean language and culture learning platform — open to everyone, everywhere, always.

OUR SLOGAN · 슬로건
"Must know the culture to truly learn language"

Korean isn't learned in isolation. It's woven through K-drama dialogue, K-pop lyrics, food names, Confucian honorifics, and everyday city life. We teach the language alongside the culture that gives it meaning.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT · 특징
🆓

Free Forever

Every lesson, quiz, and guide is completely free — no subscription, no paywall.

🚪

No Sign-Up

No account, no email, no password. Open the site and start learning immediately.

💾

Private Progress

Your progress lives in your browser's local storage — private, offline, and never shared.

🎵

Culture-First

Language in real cultural context — not just textbook sentences, but K-pop, food, travel, and news.
